# Quick Check

Run this check before shipping any page, asset, story, or implementation.

## Brief

- State the subject in one sentence without branding language.
- List the real evidence.
- Choose three or four characteristics: timeless, authentic, masculine, expensive, honest, reliable, capable.
- Choose only the reference systems that solve the problem.
- Name what should lead.
- Remove anything that does not improve understanding.

## Final test

1. Would this still look right after ten years of real use?
2. Could a real person actually use it?
3. Is the story specific to this company, project, or person?
4. Is there evidence behind the mood?
5. Does the hierarchy make the first reading step obvious?
6. Does the finish disappear behind the work?
7. Could somebody else have made this? If yes, what remains too generic?

## Guardrail

Remove fake distressing, costume nostalgia, Western clichés, sepia sentiment, mood without evidence, generic lifestyle stock, atmospheric gradients, unnecessary rounded containers, motion that substitutes for hierarchy, and SaaS polish for its own sake.

# Portfolio evidence and direction QA

## Design scope

- Change classification: KEEP the existing visual system; REFINE the evidence
  hierarchy and case-study pacing; REMOVE the ambiguous experience rail.
- Why this is the least disruptive effective classification: the established
  typography, palette, editorial grid, responsive-image pipeline, static export,
  and interaction model already work. The portfolio needed broader proof of
  direction, not a new identity.
- Pages/components/case studies affected: homepage, SANDPAPER feature, all four
  case-study direction records, and the TAP, Wild Feathers, and Wimberly Guitars
  artifact sequences.
- Evidence and permission sources: Preston's August 12, 2026 clearance; existing
  repository records and images; current public TAP and Wimberly surfaces; and
  the current Wild Feathers review archive.
